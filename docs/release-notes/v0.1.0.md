First test release of NexusIRL, a free and open-source IRL streaming app for Android, ported
from [Moblin](https://github.com/eerimoq/moblin). Expect rough edges, and please report what you
find in [Issues](https://github.com/mesmerize08/NexusIRL/issues).

**Which file:** `NexusIRL-v0.1.0-arm64-v8a.apk` for practically every phone from the last several
years, or the universal APK for anything else. Android 10 or newer.

## What works

- Streaming over **SRT** (with encryption), **SRTLA** bonding and **RTMP/RTMPS**, with adaptive
  bitrate for SRT, H.264 or HEVC, up to 4K and 60 fps, landscape or portrait.
- Status bar while live: uptime, bitrate, fps, round-trip time, audio level, thermal warnings.
- Camera: lens switching, zoom presets (including ultra-wide), pinch zoom, tap-to-focus, torch,
  exposure, manual focus, shutter, ISO and white balance.
- Recording to `Movies/NexusIRL` and snapshots to `Pictures/NexusIRL`.
- Scenes and widgets: text (time, timers, bitrate and more), image, QR code, map, chat, browser.
- Quick buttons, including mic and bitrate pickers and video effects.
- Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat (read without signing in), Twitch alerts with sign-in.
- Remote control compatible with Moblin's assistant, plus a web remote control on the local
  network (protected by an access link).
- Moblink: lend this phone's mobile data to a streamer, or use other phones as relays.
- Settings import from iOS Moblin (`.moblinSettings`), with stream keys stored encrypted.

## Tested so far

Checked on a Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra (Android 17) against local test receivers: 15-minute 1080p30
and 5-minute 1080p60 runs without dropped frames or audio drift, recording, both landscape
directions and portrait, camera controls, and the web remote control.

**Not tested yet:** bonding over a real mobile network, Moblink between two phones, interop with an
iPhone running Moblin, and phones other than the S26 Ultra. If you try any of these, a report is
very welcome.

## Known limitations

- The app does not reduce frame rate or resolution when the phone gets hot. Long 1080p60 streams
  can make a phone uncomfortably warm; use a cooling case or 30 fps.
- Some upstream Moblin features are not ported yet (replays, face effects, DJI/GoPro control, RTMP
  and SRT ingest servers, and more). See `docs/PORT_PLAN.md`.
