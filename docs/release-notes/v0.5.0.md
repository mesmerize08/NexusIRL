This release adds OBS remote control, Opus audio, Kick sign-in and many more Twitch and Kick
alerts, and makes bonded IRL streaming hold its bitrate on the road. It also closes every finding
of an independent pre-release security and reliability audit.

**Which file:** `NexusIRL-v0.5.0-arm64-v8a.apk` for practically every phone from the last several
years (about a third of the universal APK's size), or the universal APK for anything else. It
installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings.

**Twitch users:** sign in to Twitch again in the stream's settings to get the new alerts (hype
trains, polls, predictions and more). Your current sign-in keeps working with the alerts it has.

## New

- **OBS remote control** (Streams → edit → OBS remote control, OBS 28+): switch scenes, start and
  stop OBS streaming and recording, mute inputs and fix the stream's source from the new OBS
  quick button. Going live and ending can start and stop OBS, and OBS switches to a BRB scene
  while the phone's stream is reconnecting.
- **Opus audio** for streams (Streams → edit → Audio → Codec), over SRT, SRTLA and RTMP when the
  server accepts it. Recordings stay AAC.
- **Kick:** sign in to send chat messages and change the title and category; Kick alerts
  (subscriptions, gifts, rewards, hosts, Kicks) pop up and show in chat.
- **Twitch:** hype trains, polls and predictions as banners and in chat; watch streaks, Prime and
  upgraded subscriptions, shared chat partners' alerts, ad break toasts and shoutouts.
- **Torch brightness** on Android 15+ phones that support it, also from chat
  (`!moblin torch level`).
- **Stream editor:** "Paste stream key or URL", and saved keys are shown only after your
  fingerprint, face or screen lock.

## Fixed

- **Bitrate on the road:** the Galaxy S26 Ultra sent 7–14 Mbps for a 5 Mbps stream while driving,
  overloading the connection. The video now stays within 1.2 times its bitrate, as in Moblin; in
  the next field test the server lost 16 late packets in 14 minutes instead of 1,896 in 8.
- **Moblink with an iPhone** over a hotspot (IPv6) stayed on "Connecting"; it now bonds.
- **Car Bluetooth** became the microphone for whole streams. "Bluetooth output only" is now on
  by default, as in Moblin, and the microphone you choose is remembered.
- **Another app taking the camera** (the Camera app) froze the picture until the camera was
  switched by hand; it now comes back by itself.
- **No more freezes** of up to 8 seconds when going live, ending or stopping a recording.
- A failed recording (for example with storage full) no longer says "Recording saved".
- A large or broken colour LUT, for example in shared settings, no longer crashes the app.

## Security

From an independent pre-release audit (`docs/audit/` in the repository):

- Every password, key and token in the settings is kept encrypted, also those from iOS settings
  NexusIRL does not use itself, and "Export without keys" leaves all of them out. Settings from
  earlier versions are protected the first time this version starts.
- Web remote control, remote control and Moblink are hardened against overload and oversized
  messages, and accept only devices on the local network.
- Copied secrets are hidden from keyboards and cleared after a minute; remote settings imports
  need your yes on the phone (off by default).

## Tested

- Galaxy S26 Ultra: field tests on 1 and 2 November 2026 (mobile data, driving, bonding with an
  iPhone as Moblink relay, the Camera app taking the camera), each checked against the server's
  recording.
- The emulator: going live, ending, recording and leaving the app in quick succession.
- Upgrading: the published v0.4.0 updated in place to this release's signed APK (version code 6
  to 73), keeping its streams and stream keys.
- About 580 unit and interoperability test runs, including streaming to ffmpeg over RTMP, SRT and
  SRTLA, and Opus over MPEG-TS and RTMP.
