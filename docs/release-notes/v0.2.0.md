The second test release adds Twitch alerts on screen and Twitch stream title and category, and
fixes a dropped frame per second while a remote control showed the preview. It is the first
release tested live on Twitch, on a second Android phone and against iOS Moblin.

**Which file:** `NexusIRL-v0.2.0-arm64-v8a.apk` for practically every phone from the last several
years, or the universal APK for anything else. It installs over v0.1.x and keeps your settings.

## New

- **Twitch alerts on screen:** follows, subscriptions, gift subscriptions, channel point rewards,
  raids and cheers pop up one at a time while you are signed in to Twitch. Each stream chooses
  which alerts pop up and which appear in chat (Settings → Streams → your stream → Twitch alerts).
- **Twitch title and category:** see and change them in the stream's settings, with category
  search. Changes go to Twitch only after you confirm. **Sign in to Twitch again once** to grant
  the extra permission this needs.
- **Remote control over the internet with your own relay:** `server/relay` runs Moblin's
  open-source relay with automatic HTTPS. [docs/remote-control.md](https://github.com/mesmerize08/NexusIRL/blob/main/docs/remote-control.md)
  explains every way to connect, including Tailscale and the same Wi-Fi.

## Fixed

- While a remote control (Moblin's assistant or the web remote) showed the preview, the stream
  lost one frame every second.
- Twitch alerts ignored the alert settings of streams imported from iOS.
- The relay part of a remote control address (its bridge ID) is now stored encrypted and left out
  of exports and logs, like stream keys. Existing settings are converted when the app starts.
- After approving the Twitch sign-in on the phone, Twitch's page can stay blank; the app now says
  to come back to it.
- Smaller fixes: a "1 stream(s)" message and a warning logged whenever the camera closed.

## Tested

- **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** (Android 17): live on Twitch for 5 minutes over RTMPS with H.264
  (picture, sound, chat, a follow alert, title and category changes); remote control from an
  iPhone running Moblin, including going live and ending the stream.
- **Huawei P30 Pro** (Android 10, Kirin 980): this release's predecessor installed from the
  published APK, a setup link import and a 1080p30 H.265 stream with every frame arriving.
- **iOS Moblin interop:** iPhone settings export imported with its stream, scenes and widgets;
  Moblin's assistant controlling NexusIRL.
- Still untested: bonding over real mobile networks and Moblink between phones (both need mobile
  coverage away from the developer's desk).
