<p>NexusIRL is a free, open-source app for live streaming from your phone while you are out and about ("IRL" streaming). It is an Android port of Moblin, the iOS streaming app.</p>

<p><b>Early development.</b> NexusIRL already streams camera and microphone, but many features are still to come.</p>

<p>What works today:</p>
<ul>
<li>Streaming over SRT (with AES encryption), RTMP and RTMPS</li>
<li>SRTLA bonding: send one stream over cellular, Wi-Fi and Ethernet at once, for a stream that survives losing a connection</li>
<li>Adaptive bitrate that follows your network</li>
<li>H.264 and HEVC (on phones with a hardware HEVC encoder)</li>
<li>Automatic reconnect, external microphones, and a warning when the phone gets too hot</li>
</ul>

<p>Privacy and security:</p>
<ul>
<li>No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app talks only to the servers you choose.</li>
<li>Stream keys are hidden on screen, blocked from screenshots, and kept out of logs.</li>
<li>Works without Google Play Services.</li>
</ul>

<p>NexusIRL is based on Moblin by Erik Moqvist (MIT License). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Moblin project.</p>
