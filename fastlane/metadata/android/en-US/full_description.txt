<p>NexusIRL is a free, open-source app for live streaming from your phone while you are out and about ("IRL" streaming). It is an Android port of Moblin, the iOS streaming app, and works with Moblin's settings files, remote control and Moblink relays.</p>

<p><b>Early test releases (0.x).</b> Streaming, scenes, recording, chat and remote control are tested on real phones; bonding over real mobile networks is being field tested.</p>

<p>Streaming:</p>
<ul>
<li>SRT (with AES encryption), RTMP and RTMPS, including enhanced RTMP for HEVC</li>
<li>SRTLA bonding: one stream over mobile data, Wi-Fi and Ethernet at once, so it survives losing a connection</li>
<li>Adaptive bitrate that follows your network (Moblin's and BELABOX's algorithms)</li>
<li>Multi streaming: the same RTMP stream to several servers at once</li>
<li>H.264 and HEVC up to 4K, automatic reconnect, external microphones</li>
<li>Moblink: lend this phone's mobile data to a streamer, or bond other phones' data into your stream</li>
</ul>

<p>Production:</p>
<ul>
<li>Scenes with text, timer, location, image, QR code, map, chat and browser widgets</li>
<li>LUTs and effects, zoom presets and manual camera controls</li>
<li>Recording to MP4 while streaming or on its own, and snapshots (also to Discord)</li>
<li>Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat with emotes, and Twitch and Kick alerts</li>
<li>Moblin's chat bot: control the stream with !moblin commands from Twitch and Kick chat</li>
<li>Remote control from iOS Moblin, another phone or a browser on the same network</li>
</ul>

<p>Privacy and security:</p>
<ul>
<li>No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app talks only to the servers and platforms you choose.</li>
<li>Stream keys and sign-ins are encrypted on the phone, hidden on screen, blocked from screenshots and kept out of logs and exports.</li>
<li>Local remote-control servers are off by default and need a password or access link.</li>
<li>Works without Google Play Services.</li>
</ul>

<p>Chat, alerts and sign-in use Twitch, Kick and YouTube, which are not free software.</p>
